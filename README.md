### ⭐ Featured projects

**🐾 [Find Your Pet](https://github.com/berguntin/find-your-pet)**
Lost & found pet board born at a charity hackathon after the 2024 Valencia floods. Semantic and photo search with multimodal embeddings, AI moderation and form pre-fill from a photo.
`Nuxt` `TypeScript` `Postgres/pgvector` `Redis` `OpenAI` `Gemini`

**🧾 [Bill-Splitter](https://github.com/berguntin/bill-splitter)** · [Live demo](https://bill-splitterweb-production.up.railway.app/)
Snap a receipt, mark who had what, and everyone sees exactly what they owe, to the cent. Claude Haiku reads the receipt and falls back to Sonnet when the result looks unreliable.
`Vue 3` `TypeScript` `Node/Express` `MongoDB` `Playwright` `Claude API`

**⛵ [Sail Trim Simulator](https://github.com/berguntin/sail-trim-simulator)** · [Live demo](https://sail-trim-simulator.vercel.app/)
Interactive sail trim simulator. Adjust sheets, traveller or backstay and see the effect on sail shape (2D/3D), speed, heel and VMG, using real ORC polars.
`Vue 3` `TypeScript` `Pinia` `Three.js` `Vitest`

**📣 [Affiliates Manager](https://github.com/berguntin/affiliates-manager)**
Mobile-first back office for Amazon deal channels on Telegram and WhatsApp. Deal detection, a review queue, and AI-written copy that is validated before it's published.
`Nuxt 4` `Cloudflare Workers + D1` `Drizzle` `Vitest` `Anthropic API`

**💊 Apotheke** · *in progress*
Finds the nearest on-duty pharmacy, with data refreshed daily from each province's official pharmacists' association.
